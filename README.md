# 5-Minute

**Look good. Feel good. Keep the house running — in minutes.**

5-Minute is a YouTube channel for busy women who don't have an hour for anything. Beauty, family and home aren't three separate channels here — they're one life, and this is one channel. Every video answers the same question:

> *"What's the fastest version of this that still actually works?"*

Market: **United States** · Language: **American English** · Formats: **Shorts + long-form**

---

## The through-line

| Pillar | What it covers | The promise |
|---|---|---|
| **Look good** | Fast makeup, skincare, hair | Polished in minutes, not an hour |
| **Feel good** | Mental load, self-care, mindset | Small habits that keep you sane |
| **Run the house** | Routines, kids, laundry, dinner, organizing | Systems that run themselves |

Every Short feeds a long video. Every long video is chopped back into Shorts. Same woman, same voice, same rhythm: **real, quick, kind, no guilt.**

## How to use these scripts

1. **Read the hook out loud.** If it doesn't sound like you, rewrite it in your own words — the structure matters more than the exact sentences.
2. **Film the shot list first**, voiceover second. Most Shorts work best as B-roll + voiceover.
3. **Swap in your own products.** Product mentions are placeholders by category ("tinted moisturizer," "cream blush") so you're never stuck promoting something you don't use.
4. **Pin the comment** suggested at the bottom of each script — it drives replies, which drive reach.
5. **Link every Short to its long video** using YouTube's "Related video" feature.

## Publishing rhythm

- **Shorts:** 3–4 per week
- **Long videos:** 1 per week
- See [`channel/upload-plan.md`](channel/upload-plan.md) for the full 6-week calendar.

## A note on health and skincare content

Skincare and wellness scripts are written as personal experience, not medical advice. Keep the on-screen disclaimer ("What works for me — check with your dermatologist") and disclose any sponsored or gifted products per FTC guidelines (#ad / "paid partnership" toggle).

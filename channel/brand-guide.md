# 5-Minute — Brand Guide

## Who she is (the viewer)
- Women 28–45 in the US, usually moms, often working (in or out of the home)
- Time-poor, not effort-averse — she *wants* to look and feel good, she just has 7 minutes
- Tired of "perfect" content. Wants real kitchens, real kids, real mornings.

## Who you are (on camera)
The friend who has it *mostly* together and will tell you exactly how — fast.

**Voice:** warm, direct, a little funny, zero guilt.
**Never:** preachy, perfect, "just wake up at 5 a.m.," shaming.

## Recurring phrases (use them often — they become the brand)
- "Here's the 5-minute version."
- "Good enough is the goal."
- "Future-you will thank you."
- "Steal this."
- "Real house, real kids, real mornings."
- Sign-off: **"That's it. That's the 5 minutes. Go live your life."**

## Format rules
- **Shorts:** hook in the first 2 seconds, visible timer on screen, ends with a loop or a pointer to the long video.
- **Long videos:** promise + payoff in the first 30 seconds, chapters every 1–3 minutes, one clear takeaway per chapter.
- Always show a **timer** (corner overlay) when doing a timed task. It's the channel's signature.

## Visual look
- **Colors:** warm blush (#E8B4A6), soft cream (#F7F1EA), deep espresso (#3B2A25), one pop of coral (#FF6F59) for timers and arrows
- **Font:** a clean rounded sans (e.g. Poppins / Montserrat) — bold for titles
- **Lighting:** natural window light, morning feel
- **Set:** your actual bathroom, kitchen and laundry area — tidied, not staged

## Thumbnails
- Your face (real expression), one big number ("3 MIN", "10 MIN"), max 3 words
- Before/after split for beauty videos
- Coral timer icon in the corner = instantly recognizable

## Content pillars & ratio
| Pillar | Share of uploads |
|---|---|
| Look good (beauty, hair, skin) | 40% |
| Run the house (routines, kids, food, home) | 40% |
| Feel good (mental load, self-care) | 20% |

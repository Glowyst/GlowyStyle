# Faceless Guide — 5-Minute without showing your face

Every script in this repo has a **"Faceless version"** section with a faceless hook and shot swaps. This guide covers the general rules.

## The formula
**Hands + home + timer + voiceover.**
Your hands, your real rooms, the coral timer in the corner and a warm voice. That's the whole look.

## 6 faceless camera angles
1. **Top-down** — phone arm above the counter/table (cooking, products, planning)
2. **Fixed wide** — tripod in the corner of a room (time-lapses, resets)
3. **POV** — phone held at chest height (routines, walking through the house)
4. **From behind** — back of head/shoulders (hair, mirror shots)
5. **Body-part crop** — eyes only, lips only, hands only (makeup, skincare)
6. **Detail close-up** — textures, steam, pouring, clicking a pen (emotional moments)

## Your voice
| Option | When |
|---|---|
| **Your own voice** (recommended) | Builds trust, especially for mental-load and parenting topics |
| **AI voice** (e.g. ElevenLabs, American English) | When you're not comfortable recording in English yet; pick one voice and always use the same one |

## AI tools that can help
- **CapCut** — editing, auto-captions, script-to-video with stock footage
- **ElevenLabs** — natural American-English voiceover
- **InVideo AI / Pictory / Fliki** — generate a draft video from a script with stock footage
- **Canva** — thumbnails, text cards, animated graphics (weekly grid, breathing box)

## Rules to stay safe on YouTube
- **Add something original to every video.** Mass-produced, near-identical videos can lose monetization. Your own footage + your own tips = original.
- **Disclose realistic AI content.** When uploading, check "altered or synthetic content" if a realistic person, voice or scene was generated by AI. (A clearly AI voice reading your script over your own footage usually doesn't need it, but disclose when in doubt.)
- **Use licensed stock footage and music only** (from the tool's own library or YouTube Audio Library).

## Faceless thumbnails
No face? Then use: one strong object (laundry mountain, product line-up, a clock at 5:45), a big number ("10 MIN"), max 3 words and the coral timer icon.

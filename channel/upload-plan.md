# 6-Week Upload Plan

Long videos go out **Sunday morning** (8–10 a.m. ET — planning mode for the week).
Shorts go out **Mon / Wed / Fri** around 7 a.m. or 8 p.m. ET (before the rush / after bedtime).

| Week | Long video (Sun) | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | L1 — Full 10-minute morning routine | S01 3-min makeup · S04 Kids out the door · S08 2-min hair |
| 2 | L3 — Weekly home-organizing system | S03 Evening reset · S06 One-basket laundry · S09 15-min dinner |
| 3 | L2 — Polished in your 30s and 40s | S02 Skincare swap · re-cut from L2 · re-cut from L1 |
| 4 | L4 — 20 cheap family activities | S05 5-min family game · 2× re-cuts from L4 (one activity each) |
| 5 | L5 — Mental load reset | S07 "Good enough" rule · S10 Self-care between pick-ups · re-cut from L5 |
| 6 | Community post + best-of Short compilation | 3× re-cuts from best-performing long video |

## Re-cut rule
Every long video produces at least **3 extra Shorts**: one per strongest chapter. Title them as standalone tips.

## Weekly checklist
- [ ] Thumbnail A/B test (YouTube "Test & compare")
- [ ] Pin a question comment within the first hour
- [ ] Reply to the first 20 comments
- [ ] Link each Short to its long video ("Related video")
- [ ] Add the long video to its playlist: *Look Good*, *Feel Good* or *Run the House*

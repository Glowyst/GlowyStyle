## Key script moments
**Skin (2:45):**
> "Three steps. Clean, moisturize, protect. If you only do one thing, make it sunscreen — it's the best anti-aging product you'll ever own and it takes 20 seconds."

**When it goes wrong (10:15):**
> "Some mornings the toddler spills the cereal at minute four and the whole plan's gone. My 90-second Plan B: SPF, cream blush on cheeks and lips, hair in a claw clip. You still look like you tried. Because you did."

**Sign-off:**
> "That's it. That's the 5 — okay, the 10 — minutes. Go live your life."

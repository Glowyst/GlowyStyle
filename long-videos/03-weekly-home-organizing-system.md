## Intro (0:00–1:00)
> "I used to spend every Sunday catching up. Laundry mountain, empty fridge, a house that looked like a tornado lived in it. And Monday it started all over again.

> Now my house runs on about 20 minutes a day and one 30-minute session on Sunday. Not perfect — running. I'm going to give you the whole system: the daily basics, one focus zone per day, laundry, meals and the Sunday reset. Steal all of it."

## Key script moments
**The rule (1:00):**
> "The whole system comes down to one idea: maintain, don't catch up. Ten minutes a day keeps the mess small enough that it never needs a whole Saturday."

**Getting the family on board (13:00):**
> "Kids 3 to 5: toys in bins, clothes in the hamper. 6 to 9: set the table, put away their own laundry. 10 and up: unload the dishwasher, one bathroom sink. And everybody does the 5-minute timer tidy together before screens. It's a game, not a punishment."

**When it falls apart (14:30):**
> "Sick kids, work deadline, whatever — drop the zones, keep the evening reset. That's the floor. As long as the floor holds, you're never starting from zero."

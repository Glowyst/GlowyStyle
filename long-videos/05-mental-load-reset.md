## Intro (0:00–1:00)
> "You know that feeling when you're lying in bed and your brain goes: did I sign the permission slip? Are we out of milk? When's the dentist? That's the mental load — the invisible to-do list that never turns off.
>
> I can't make it disappear. But I've found seven simple habits that make it lighter. None of them take more than five minutes, and they've honestly changed how I feel every day."

## Key script moments
**Habit 4 (6:15):**
> "The most exhausting part isn't buying the gift. It's remembering the party, RSVPing, knowing your kid's friend loves unicorns, and checking the gift is wrapped. So when you hand something off — hand off all of it. Including the remembering."

**Habit 7 — the Sunday check-in script (on screen):**
1. What's on the calendar this week?
2. Anything we need to buy or pay?
3. Who owns which task?
4. One thing each of us needs this week to feel okay.

**When it's more (11:45):**
> "These habits help with the load. But if you feel overwhelmed most days, can't sleep, or just don't feel like yourself — please talk to your doctor or a therapist. Asking for help is the most 'together' thing you can do."

**Sign-off:**
> "Pick one habit. Just one. That's it. That's the 5 minutes. Go live your life."

# Short 01 — 3-minute makeup for a tired morning

**Length:** 50–60 sec · **Pillar:** Look good · **Feeds into:** Long 1, Long 2

## Title options

## Hook (0–2 sec)
*(Bare, tired face, coffee in hand, timer at 3:00)*
"I slept five hours. Watch me fake eight — in three minutes."

## Script
| Time | Voiceover | On screen / shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "I slept five hours. Watch me fake eight — in three minutes." | Close-up, timer starts **3:00** |
| 0:03 | "Step one: color corrector or concealer — under the eyes only, inner corner out. That's where tired lives." | Peach corrector → concealer, tap with ring finger. Text: **UNDER-EYE ONLY** |
| 0:13 | "Step two: tinted moisturizer or skin tint. Fingers, middle of the face, blend out. No sponge, no brush." | Text: **CENTER → OUT** |
| 0:22 | "Step three — the one that does the most: cream blush. High on the cheeks, then whatever's left goes on the lips." | Tap blush on cheeks, swipe lips. Text: **1 PRODUCT, 2 JOBS** |
| 0:32 | "Step four: brow gel. Brush up. Done brows make you look awake." | Clear/tinted brow gel |
| 0:39 | "Step five: one coat of mascara, top lashes only. Curl first if you've got 10 extra seconds." | Mascara. Timer ~0:25 left |
| 0:48 | "Before… after. Nobody needs to know about the five hours." | Split screen before/after |
| 0:54 | "Full routine — with the kids — is on my channel. That's the 5 minutes." | Text: **FULL MORNING ROUTINE ↗** |

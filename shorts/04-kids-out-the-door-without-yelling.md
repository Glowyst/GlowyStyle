# Short 04 — How I get kids out the door without yelling

## Script
| Time | Voiceover | On screen / shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "That used to be every morning at our house. Here's what fixed it." | Calm face, coffee |

| 0:42 | "And the secret bonus: everything was ready the night before." | Launch pad by door |

# Short 06 — One-basket laundry system for busy homes

**Length:** 50–60 sec · **Pillar:** Run the house · **Feeds into:** Long 3

## Title options

## Hook (0–2 sec)

"This was my couch every Sunday. Now I never have a laundry pile. One basket."

## Script
| Time | Voiceover | On screen / shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "This was my couch every Sunday. Now I never have a pile. One basket." | Laundry mountain |
| 0:05 | "Rule one: one load a day. Not a laundry day — a laundry habit." | Text: **1 LOAD / DAY** |
| 0:12 | "Rule two: start it in the morning. Switch it at lunch or after school. Fold it during one show tonight." | Text: **START · SWITCH · FOLD** |
| 0:22 | "Rule three: one basket, one person. Monday is kid one, Tuesday kid two, Wednesday towels… No sorting by color across the whole family." | Weekly schedule graphic |
| 0:34 | "Rule four: clean clothes go back in the same basket and straight to that person's room. Kids old enough put away their own." | Basket to kid's room |
| 0:43 | "One basket, one load, one person. No mountain." | Empty couch |
| 0:49 | "Want my whole weekly home system? It's on my channel." | Text: **WEEKLY HOME SYSTEM ↗** |

## Tip for the voiceover/caption
Wash cold and use mixed-color loads if you're nervous about sorting — most everyday clothes are fine. Keep a small "delicates" bag for the exceptions.

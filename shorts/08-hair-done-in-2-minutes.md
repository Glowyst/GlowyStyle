# Short 08 — Hair that looks done in 2 minutes

**Length:** 45–55 sec · **Pillar:** Look good · **Feeds into:** Long 1, Long 2

## Title options
1. Hair that looks done in 2 minutes
2. Second-day hair → "did you get a blowout?" in 2 min
3. The 2-minute hair trick for busy mornings

## Hook (0–2 sec)
*(Flat, second-day hair, timer 2:00)*
"Second-day hair, two minutes, and people will ask if you got a blowout."

## Script
| Time | Voiceover | On screen / shot |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Second-day hair, two minutes, and people will ask if you got a blowout." | Flat hair, timer **2:00** |
| 0:04 | "Step one: dry shampoo at the roots — then wait 30 seconds. That's where everyone goes wrong." | Spray roots. Text: **WAIT 30 SEC** |
| 0:14 | "While you wait: brush your teeth. Multitasking." | Quick funny cut |
| 0:18 | "Step two: massage it in, flip your head over, shake it out. Instant volume." | Head flip |
| 0:25 | "Step three: two-section twist. Split your hair in half, twist each side away from your face, clip for 60 seconds while you do something else." | Twist + clips. Text: **TWIST AWAY FROM FACE** |
| 0:38 | "Step four: unclip, shake out, a tiny bit of hair oil on the ends." | Release, soft waves |
| 0:45 | "Done. Two minutes. Looks intentional." | Final look, timer **0:00** |
| 0:49 | "My whole 10-minute morning routine is on my channel." | Text: **FULL MORNING ROUTINE ↗** |

Works best on straight to wavy hair, shoulder length or longer. For a follow-up Short: version for short hair / curly hair.

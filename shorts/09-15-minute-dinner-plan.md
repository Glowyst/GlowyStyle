# Short 09 — 15-minute dinner plan for a hectic night

**Length:** 55–60 sec · **Pillar:** Run the house · **Feeds into:** Long 3

## Title options
1. 15-minute dinner plan for a hectic night

## Hook (0–2 sec)
*(Clock shows 5:45 p.m., kids asking "what's for dinner?")*
"It's 5:45, everyone's hungry, and I have no plan. Except this one."

## The formula: 1 protein + 1 carb + 1 veg + 1 sauce
Keep these always in stock = dinner in 15 minutes.

## Combo cards (on screen)
1. **Taco bowls:** ground turkey + tortilla chips/rice + bagged lettuce + salsa
2. **Pasta night:** frozen meatballs + pasta + spinach + jarred marinara
3. **Breakfast-for-dinner:** eggs + toast + cherry tomatoes + shredded cheese

## Pantry "always" list (for description)
Rotisserie chicken or frozen protein · microwave rice / pasta / tortillas · frozen veggies · 3 sauces (teriyaki, marinara, salsa)

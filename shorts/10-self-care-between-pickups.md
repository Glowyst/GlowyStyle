# Short 10 — A tiny self-care ritual between school pick-ups

**Length:** 40–55 sec · **Pillar:** Feel good · **Feeds into:** Long 5

## Title options
1. A tiny self-care ritual between school pick-ups
2. The 5 minutes in the car that keep me sane
3. Self-care for moms who have zero time

## Hook (0–2 sec)
*(In the parked car, school pick-up line, engine off)*
"I'm 10 minutes early for pick-up. Here's what I do instead of scrolling."

## The ritual: "Car-Line Reset" (5 minutes)
1. Phone face down
2. 1 minute of box breathing (in 4 – hold 4 – out 4 – hold 4)
3. Hand cream + lip balm, slowly
4. Water + a snack that's just for you
5. One song you love, out loud
